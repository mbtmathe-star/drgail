About Dr Gail Motlhaudi-Banda

Entrepreneurship expert. Mindset strategist.

Author, speaker, academic, internationally certified life coach and mindset strategist committed to helping people transform their thinking and build meaningful success.

Dr Gail Motlhaudi-Banda is committed to helping people discover their purpose, strengthen their mindset and create meaningful, sustainable success.

Holding a PhD in Entrepreneurship, Dr Gail is an international award-winning academic who has dedicated her career to developing entrepreneurs, empowering students, supporting business growth and equipping leaders with the mindset needed to thrive in today’s changing world.

Her work bridges the gap between academic research and practical application, making entrepreneurship accessible, relevant and impactful.

Bridging academic research and practical application.

As the founder of GGM Coaching, Dr Gail delivers transformational coaching programmes focused on mindset development, entrepreneurship, leadership and personal growth.

She is also the author of Rewire Your Mind in 21 Days and Mind Power 4 Teens Journal, practical resources that inspire readers to overcome limiting beliefs, develop resilience and pursue purposeful living.

Empowering women and mentoring young entrepreneurs.

Beyond academia and business, Dr Gail is passionate about empowering women, mentoring young entrepreneurs and contributing to entrepreneurship development across South Africa and beyond.

Her international engagements continue to broaden her perspective while strengthening her commitment to creating lasting impact through education, innovation and leadership.

Purpose. Excellence. Impact.
